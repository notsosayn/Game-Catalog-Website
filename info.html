Skip the Malware

Where to actually get your games—and how to spot fake download traps.

Trusted Official Platforms

  • Steam & Epic Games Store: The main PC hubs for titles like The Last of Us, God of War, Detroit: Become Human, and Little Nightmares II—with automatic updates and cloud saves.
  • GOG (Good Old Games): Best for DRM-free games where you own the offline installer without needing a background launcher.
  • Official Publisher Stores: First-party launchers like the Rockstar Games Launcher, PlayStation PC, and the Microsoft Store come straight from the developers.

Red Flags to Avoid

  • "Free" Paid AAA Games: Sites offering $60 releases for free almost always bundle trojans, crypto-miners, or ransomware.
  • Fake Download Banners: Ad-heavy pages surround real links with flashing "DOWNLOAD NOW" buttons that trigger unwanted .exe installers.
  • Password-Protected Archives: Scammers lock .zip or .rar files with a password so browser scanners cannot inspect them before extraction.
  • "Disable Antivirus" Prompts: Legitimate game stores never ask you to turn off Windows Defender to install a game.
  • Look-Alike Domains: Always check the URL bar for typos like steampowered-free.com instead of steampowered.com.

Quick 3-Step Safety Checklist

  1. Use Direct Links: Click through Frolik’s official store links instead of searching "free game download" on a search engine.
  2. Check Your Specs First: Run the game through Frolik’s "Can my PC run it?" checker before downloading 70+ GB of files.
  3. Update Drivers Officially: Download GPU drivers strictly from NVIDIA, AMD, or Intel—never third-party driver updater tools.
